# F6.5 & F6.5.1 GEO / AI SEARCH IMPLEMENTATION REPORT

**Date:** 2026-08-20
**Status:** COMPLETE (F6.5 & F6.5.1 Gates Passed)

This report details the implementation of the GEO and AI Search readiness architecture for Metro Mitra, ensuring factual entity clarity, semantic answerability, and safe geographic representation without keyword stuffing or massive programmatic page bloat.

---

## 1. Research Traceability
We established `docs/seo/10_GEO_AI_SEARCH_ARCHITECTURE.md` to trace every GEO decision directly back to the Metro Mitra SEO Blueprint. Pages are designed to answer specific human intents rather than manipulating AI algorithms. 

## 2. Entity Strategy
- Metro Mitra is established as the primary public entity and described as a gig workforce platform. Corporate/legal relationships are intentionally conservative until authoritative verification supports additional public entity relationships.
- Added a dedicated `<EntityDefinition />` component to the Homepage, directly answering *What it is*, *Who it is for*, *What it provides*, and *Where it operates*.
- Fixed missing `H1` tags on the Homepage to guarantee crawler extraction.

## 3. Worker & Hirer GEO (Role/Service)
- **Role Pages:** Validated that Role Pages contain clear requirements under semantic headings (e.g., `H3: About this Role`, `H4: Requirements`). 
- **Earnings Section:** Implemented an evidence gate. **Verified earning data exists -> earnings section may render. No verified earning data -> earnings section omitted.** Earning models are not presented as a current public feature unless supported by actual data.
- **Service Pages (B2B & B2C):** Fixed missing data structures to ensure service pages render `Use Cases` and `Who is this for?` content blocks contextually under correct semantic headings, providing immediate answers to the primary user intent.
- **Content Matrices:** Established `docs/seo/10_GEO_CONTENT_MATRIX.md` defining the primary question, required answer, and supporting facts required for every template before it can become eligible.

## 4. AI-Search Readiness & Crawler Policy
- **llms.txt Positioning:** `public/llms.txt` is a supplementary machine-readable resource and is not treated as a Google ranking or indexing requirement. It does not guarantee AI visibility or citations.
- **OpenAI Crawler Accessibility:** Checked `robots.txt` generator configuration. `OAI-SearchBot` is not blocked. Public pages remain accessible to search and AI retrievers without relying on "hidden" or "AI-only" text blocks (which are explicitly asserted against in the test suite).
- Explicitly defined the Crawler Policy in the architecture doc: public pages remain open to standard and AI crawlers alike, emphasizing factual list structures and FAQs rather than hidden text or keyword walls.

## 5. Claims Safety & Evidence Model
- Established `docs/seo/11_SEO_CLAIMS_REGISTRY.md` to lock down quantitative and factual claims.
- The `test-geo-content.js` script actively prohibits deployment if forbidden unverified claims (e.g., "Pan-India", "millions of workers", salary wording) or negative entity definitions are found in the rendered HTML.
- **Evidence Model:** `not-yet-eligible` location pages are blocked from receiving bloated GEO text expansions until active workforce evidence flips them to `eligible`.

## 6. Validation
- `scripts/test-geo-content.js` was built and passed perfectly across all 33 combinatorial paths.
- It verifies:
  1. Exactly one prominent H1 per page.
  2. Absence of forbidden terms/claims (e.g., unsupported corporate relationships, wages on unverified pages).
  3. Strict entity terminology on the Homepage.
  4. Intent-specific semantic headings (`Requirements` for Roles, `Use Cases` for Services).
  5. Prevention of bloated paragraphs on `not-yet-eligible` geo stubs.

## 7. Performance & Accessibility
- The GEO content relies entirely on standard React semantic elements (H1-H4, lists, clean paragraphs) without adding heavy client-side libraries.
- The `<EntityDefinition />` is fully accessible and statically rendered during the Vite build process.

## 8. Remaining Limitations
- Earnings will remain invisible until real Jobs API data passes the evidence gate in the frontend.
- Barrackpore and Dankuni are currently used as representative GEO/location test cases. Broader geographic page expansion remains evidence-gated until verified operational data is available.
